## Purchases and billing

If an in-app subscription is offered on your device, its price and terms are displayed before confirmation by Apple. Purchases, renewals, and cancellation are handled through your Apple Account. To review or cancel a subscription, use [Apple's subscriptions settings](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). To request help with an Apple charge or refund, use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). If a purchased feature does not unlock, first confirm you are using the Apple Account that purchased it, then contact us at the address above with the app version and the error text; do not send account passwords or payment information.

## Other help

For app launch, meal-planning, or grocery-list problems, tell us which screen was open and the steps needed to reproduce the issue. We will use your report to investigate and reply through the email you send. The support contact above is available whether or not you have a subscription.
