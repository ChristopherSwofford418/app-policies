Privacy Policy

Bible Anew, formerly DailyPromise · Updated September 30, 2026. The optional account and offline-Bible features described here are part of the upcoming iOS 1.1.5 update; current 1.1.4 users may not see them yet.

Bible Anew is a Christian Bible reading and devotional app published by ClearPass Technologies. You may read the free Bible without an app account or purchase. This policy explains what happens if you use the optional Apple account and paid membership.

Reading choices stay on your device

Your focus, reading experience and path, prayer-style choice, saved Scripture, reading and prayer check-ins, and reminder settings are held in app storage on your device. Bible Anew does not upload these choices to an account or use them for advertising or behavioral analytics. If iCloud/device backup is enabled on your phone, Apple may include app data under your own backup settings. Uninstalling may remove app storage; saved passages are not synced by this app and may not be recoverable on another phone.

Optional Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in, Apple supplies an identity token and may supply an email address or an Apple private-relay email. We exchange the token with our separate DailyPromise Supabase Auth project and store the resulting account identifier and email or relay address to operate your login. An authentication session is held in your device’s secure storage. Supabase processes necessary authentication and security requests, which may include normal connection logs. We do not send your faith focus, saved verses or prayer activity to Supabase. You can sign out and permanently delete your app account in Settings; deleting it does not cancel an Apple subscription or remove locally saved reading data.

Membership and billing

Apple operates in-app purchases, recurring billing, eligibility for any introductory offer and subscription cancellation. We do not receive your credit card or payment details. The app queries Apple StoreKit for current purchase status to grant paid features, including restores for existing monthly and annual subscribers. App account sign-in is optional and separate from the Apple Account used by the App Store. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Reminders, Scripture and support

If you enable an optional reminder, it is scheduled locally by the app; it is not a server push notification. The 66-book World English Bible text and branded images ship with the iOS 1.1.5 app, so normal reading needs no web request. Contacting Help & Support opens your chosen email app; any details you choose to send are processed by your mail service and ours. Please do not include private prayer details, payment information or sensitive information in a support message.

No ads or tracking

The app has no third-party advertising or behavioral tracking SDK. Optional Apple/Supabase authentication is the only app-account service; it is not a sync service for your personal Bible activity.

Children, changes and contact

We do not knowingly solicit personal details from children; the Bible can be read without creating an account. If you believe a child created an account, contact us for deletion. Policy changes will appear here with an updated date. For privacy questions or account deletion assistance, email support@dailypromise.io.
