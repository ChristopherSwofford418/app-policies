# Aweframe — Privacy Policy

**Updated October 1, 2026.** This policy describes the Aweframe 1.0.0 TestFlight candidate. It is not an announcement of App Store approval or public release.

## What stays on your device

Moments, captions, selected photos, categories and onboarding progress are stored on your device. Aweframe copies a selected photo into the app’s private local storage so the journal can continue to show it after the photo picker closes. No Aweframe account or cloud sync is offered in this version. We do not receive your journal content on a server, use advertising trackers, or send third-party analytics from this app.

Aweframe asks for camera access only when you choose **Use camera**. It opens the system photo selector only when you choose **Choose a photo**. Your camera and photo library are not scanned in the background. The app does not request location or microphone access. If you explicitly choose **Share**, your iPhone’s system share sheet handles the destination you select; the privacy practices of that destination then apply.

## Retention and control

You can edit or delete a moment in the app. Deleting a moment removes its journal record and attempts to remove its app-owned photo copy. The original image in your photo library is not deleted. Removing the app may delete its local journal and copies. Sharing an entry is not a complete backup, and this version has no cross-device restore service. Apple or your device may provide an independent device backup according to your settings.

## Payments and external services

This version has no subscription, in-app purchase, account login, Firebase integration or Mixpanel analytics. Links to this public policy or its support page are handled outside your private journal. No health claims, diagnostic profiling or AI analysis are performed.

## Help

For a non-sensitive issue, use the [Aweframe support page](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/aweframe-support.md). Its linked GitHub issue route is public: do not post personal photos, captions, contact details or location information there. This policy will be updated before any future opt-in telemetry, synchronization or paid features are introduced.
