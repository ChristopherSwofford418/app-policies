# StaySlate — Help & Support (Pre-release)

StaySlate is a **pre-release** turnover-handoff concept for small short-stay hosts and their existing cleaners. There is no downloadable app, paid plan, reservation integration, cleaner marketplace or automated cleaning service. StaySlate is independent and is not affiliated with Airbnb, Vrbo, Turno or any booking provider; a proposed checklist cannot certify cleanliness, legal compliance or guest safety.

For general product feedback, [open a public issue in the support repository](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) with a title beginning `StaySlate:`. **Issues are public. Do not post booking dates, property locations/addresses, access codes, guest or cleaner details, photos, or other personal information.** For an urgent booking or safety issue, contact the host, cleaner, booking provider or relevant emergency service directly.

[Privacy information](stayslate-privacy.md). Functional support, data deletion and any billing instructions will be documented only after a real app is built and tested.
