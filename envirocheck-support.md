# EnviroCheck: Air & Water Score — Support

EnviroCheck is published by ClearPass Technologies, LLC. To ask a question, request help, report a problem, or send feedback, contact **[info@clearpasstechnologies.com](mailto:info@clearpasstechnologies.com?subject=EnviroCheck%20Support)**. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue occurred. Do not email passwords or payment card information.

## Common questions

**The app does not open or closes unexpectedly.** Force-close it and reopen it, check for an update in TestFlight or the App Store, and email support with your device and version details if the issue continues.

**A city or location is missing.** Check your connection and try searching by a nearby city or postal code. If you choose to use your current location, iOS will ask for permission; you can also search without granting location permission.

**A subscription does not appear.** On the app's membership screen, use **Restore Purchases** while signed in to the Apple ID that made the purchase. If the issue persists, contact support and do not send your full receipt or Apple ID password by email.

**Cancel or manage a subscription.** Open iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** and select EnviroCheck. Apple handles billing and cancellation. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

EnviroCheck's environmental information is for general information and should not be treated as a safety certification, a health diagnosis, or a substitute for official local advisories.
