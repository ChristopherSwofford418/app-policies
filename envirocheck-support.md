# EnviroCheck — Support

EnviroCheck is published by ClearPass Technologies, LLC. To ask a question, request help, report a problem or send feedback, contact **[info@clearpasstechnologies.com](mailto:info@clearpasstechnologies.com?subject=EnviroCheck%20Support)**. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the issue occurred. Do not email passwords, payment-card information, precise location coordinates or full purchase receipts.

## Common questions

**The app does not open or closes unexpectedly.** Force-close and reopen it, check for an update in TestFlight or the App Store, and contact support with the exact installed version and error if the issue continues.

**A city or reading is unavailable.** Check your network connection and search for a city or another nearby city. OpenWeatherMap may have no current air or weather result, may reject or rate-limit a request, or may be temporarily unavailable. The app should show a clear unavailable state rather than a made-up score. Refresh requests new data when available; retry later if the provider is unavailable. Do not send your exact coordinates in a support request.

**Location permission was denied.** You can still search for a city. The app asks for foreground location only if you choose **Use My Location**. To change that permission, open iOS **Settings → EnviroCheck → Location**.

**Saved places or detailed factors are unavailable.** These are optional Pro features. First make sure a real reading is available for the selected place. If you have a current subscription, try **Restore Purchases** in the app with the Apple account used for the completed purchase; a displayed purchase sheet by itself does not grant access.

**Cancel or manage an existing Monthly or Annual subscription.** Open iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** and select EnviroCheck. Apple handles billing and cancellation. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Do not send your full receipt or Apple ID password to support.

EnviroCheck reports available air-quality and current-weather information, not a certified U.S. AQI, drinking-water quality, pollen, noise, medical advice or a safety decision. Data can be incomplete or delayed. For health and safety decisions, follow official local advisories.
