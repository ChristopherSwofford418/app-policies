# Tanakh Anew · תנ״ך מחדש — Help & Support

**Current verified distribution: internal TestFlight 1.0.0/builds 1 and 2; not publicly released.** Build 1 is a free offline Jewish Tanakh reader with **no Apple account, subscription, paywall or purchase button**. Premium/Apple-account build 2 is Apple VALID in internal TestFlight but still uses RevenueCat; backend records have not verified its native login or purchase. A separate **direct Apple StoreKit build-3 source candidate has not been built or installed**. Neither app version nor the weekly subscription has been submitted for App Review.

## How to get help

For an app problem, accessibility issue, incorrect text reference or feature suggestion, [open a support issue](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) and put **Tanakh Anew** in the title. A GitHub account may be required. **Issues are public. Do not post your name, email, account ID, private reading notes, passwords, receipts or payment details.** If a future account-deletion request cannot be completed in the app, post only a generic request for a private contact route; do not include account information in the public issue.

## Reading and saved verses

Choose Hebrew or English first. All 24 traditional Jewish books, both text editions, chapter reading and search are included offline. You can change language, adjust text size and save verses locally. Saved verses and reading/study progress **do not sync across devices** in either build 1 or the build-2 candidate. Removing the app may remove local reading data.

## Membership and billing — not in build 1

The onboarding shows the premium study offer **before its optional Apple account step**. The entire Tanakh, search and saved verses remain free through **Skip and read the free Tanakh**, with no account. A real seven-day verse-anchored study path would refresh weekly for Premium Study members; its progress is local. In the unbuilt direct-StoreKit candidate, an optional Apple account is also accessible from Membership for free readers and **does not sync reading activity**.

The draft U.S. Apple subscription is **$2.99/week**, with **no free trial**. It is **not approved or available in build 1**. The future direct StoreKit paywall displays the actual Apple price for your App Store region and grants guided study only when Apple confirms a verified unexpired subscription on the device. The subscription renews weekly until canceled. To stop future renewal, manage it in **iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions** or the app's **Manage in Apple subscriptions** action; cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. **Restore purchases** rechecks the active Apple subscription. Deleting a Tanakh account or app does **not** cancel Apple billing. Build 3 still needs real-device TestFlight testing before release.

## Apple account — not in build 1

The build-3 source uses native Sign in with Apple and a separate Tanakh Supabase backend, independently of an active premium membership. The account is optional for free and paying readers. After device validation, **Membership → Delete Tanakh account** deletes its backend login after confirmation, but leaves locally stored verses and Apple billing unchanged; cancellation is separate. Never include account details in public issues.
