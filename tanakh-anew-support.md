# Tanakh Anew · תנ״ך מחדש — Help & Support

**Release status:** iOS **1.0.0/build 3** and the first weekly study subscription and subscription group are **Waiting for Apple App Review**. Tanakh is **not publicly released**; approval and public availability have not occurred. Build 3 is Apple VALID and installed in internal TestFlight, with direct Apple StoreKit and optional Sign in with Apple. Build 1 was free-reader only. Older build 2 used RevenueCat and is not the App Review binary. Public release remains manual even if Apple approves the submitted package.

## Get help safely

For an app issue, accessibility problem or incorrect text reference, [open a support issue](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) with **Tanakh Anew** in its title. A GitHub account may be needed. **Issues are public: do not post your name, email, account ID, private reading notes, passwords, receipts or payment details.** For a sensitive account-deletion issue, post only a generic request for a private contact route.

## Reading and saved verses

Select Hebrew or English. All 24 traditional Jewish books, both text editions, offline reading, chapter search and bookmarks remain free. Reading choices, saved verses and guided-study progress stay on the device; they **do not sync across devices**. Removing the app may remove local reading data.

## Optional Study membership in builds 2 and 3

Onboarding shows an optional weekly guided-study offer before the optional Apple-account step. Use **Skip and read the free Tanakh** to read without a purchase or account. Paid study is a source-backed seven-day verse path that refreshes each membership week, with completion stored locally. The U.S. Apple subscription is **$2.99/week**, with no free trial; the app shows Apple's localized storefront price. It renews weekly until canceled. Cancel via **iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions**, or the app's Apple subscription-management action; cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Deleting the app or Tanakh account does **not** cancel billing.

**Build 3 purchases are handled by Apple StoreKit directly.** After subscribing, the app must verify a current unexpired Apple entitlement before unlocking study. **Restore Purchases** rechecks the Apple subscription. The internal TestFlight user reported completing a sandbox purchase, unlocking the seven-day path and restoring access on build 3. The older build 2 uses RevenueCat and is not the intended public-release binary. If purchase or Restore fails, report the exact on-screen error without posting a receipt publicly.

## Optional Apple account in builds 2 and 3

From **Membership**, free or paying readers may choose Sign in with Apple. This establishes identity in a separate Tanakh backend if its native token exchange succeeds; it does **not** synchronize bookmarks. A build-3 TestFlight sign-in produced a verified Apple identity in the Tanakh backend before App Review submission. A signed-in reader may select **Membership → Delete Tanakh account** and confirm; backend deletion leaves local verses and any Apple subscription unchanged. Real-device account deletion has not yet been verified; if it fails, use the private-contact guidance above.
