# Tanakh Anew · תנ״ך מחדש — Help & Support

**Current distribution: internal TestFlight 1.0.0/build 1; not publicly released.** Build 1 is a free offline Jewish Tanakh reader. It has **no Apple account, subscription, paywall or purchase button**. A different premium/Apple-account source candidate has not yet been built or submitted to Apple.

## How to get help

For an app problem, accessibility issue, incorrect text reference or feature suggestion, [open a support issue](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) and put **Tanakh Anew** in the title. A GitHub account may be required. **Issues are public. Do not post your name, email, account ID, private reading notes, passwords, receipts or payment details.** If a future account-deletion request cannot be completed in the app, post only a generic request for a private contact route; do not include account information in the public issue.

## Reading and saved verses

Choose Hebrew or English first. All 24 traditional Jewish books, both text editions, chapter reading and search are included offline. You can change language, adjust text size and save verses locally. Saved verses and reading/study progress **do not sync across devices** in either current build 1 or the unbuilt candidate. Removing the app may remove local reading data.

## Membership and billing — not in build 1

The future candidate shows the premium study offer **before Apple account login**. The entire Tanakh, search and saved verses remain free through **Skip and read the free Tanakh**, with no account. A real seven-day verse-anchored study path would refresh weekly for Premium Study members; its progress is local. A member may optionally sign in with Apple after purchasing or restoring premium. This account identifies the user across devices but **does not sync reading activity**.

The draft U.S. Apple subscription is **$2.99/week**, with **no free trial**. It is **not approved or available in build 1**. Any future paywall must display the actual Apple price for your App Store region. The subscription renews weekly until canceled. To stop future renewal, manage the subscription in **iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions** or the app's **Manage in Apple subscriptions** action; cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. An existing active purchase can be checked with **Restore purchases**. Deleting a Tanakh account or app does **not** cancel Apple billing. Purchases and restores still need real-device TestFlight testing before release.

## Apple account — not in build 1

The candidate uses native Sign in with Apple and a separate Tanakh Supabase backend, only after an active premium membership. The account is optional even for paying members. In a future verified build, **Membership → Delete Tanakh account** deletes its backend login after confirmation, but leaves locally stored verses and Apple billing unchanged; cancellation is separate. Never include account details in public issues.
