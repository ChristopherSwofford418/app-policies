# Tanakh Anew · תנ״ך מחדש — Help & Support

**Internal TestFlight 1.0.0/build 1; not publicly released.** Tanakh Anew currently offers a standalone offline Jewish Tanakh reader. Build 1 does **not** offer an Apple account, purchase or paid membership.

## How to get help

For an app problem, accessibility issue, incorrect text reference, or feature suggestion, [open a support issue](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) and put **Tanakh Anew** in the title. A free GitHub account may be required. **Issues are public:** do not include personal data, private reading notes, passwords, device identifiers, or financial information. A private support route may be added before public release.

## Reading and saved verses

Choose Hebrew or English on the first screen. All 24 traditional Jewish books, the chapter reader and search are included offline. You can change language later, switch editions in a chapter, adjust text size, and save verses locally. Your saved verses do not synchronize across devices in build 1. If the app is deleted, local saved data may be removed under your device’s app-data rules.

## Membership and billing

There is **no subscription, free trial or purchase button in TestFlight build 1**. No credit card is required to read the complete Tanakh. A separate, **unbuilt** source candidate is being developed with an optional seven-day guided study path that refreshes each week, optional Apple Sign-In and an Apple-billed weekly membership. The draft U.S. Apple subscription is **$2.99/week**; it has **not been submitted or approved**, and no purchase is available in build 1. Any later release must provide the actual local App Store price, a working purchase and Restore flow, cancellation through Apple Subscriptions, and updated privacy/terms information. The complete Tanakh text, search and saved verses will remain free.

See [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/tanakh-anew-privacy.md) and [Text Editions](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/tanakh-anew-sources.md).
