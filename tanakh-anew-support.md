# Tanakh Anew · תנ״ך מחדש — Help & Support

**Pre-release app candidate.** Tanakh Anew is a standalone offline Jewish Tanakh reader; no App Store build or purchase is available yet.

## How to get help

For an app problem, accessibility issue, incorrect text reference, or feature suggestion, [open a support issue](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) and put **Tanakh Anew** in the title. A free GitHub account may be required. **Issues are public:** do not include personal data, private reading notes, passwords, device identifiers, or financial information. A private email support route may be added before App Review.

## Reading and saved verses

Choose Hebrew or English on the first screen. The 24 books, chapter reader, and search are included offline. You can change language later, switch editions in a chapter, adjust text size, and save verses locally. Your saved verses do not synchronize across devices in this candidate. If the app is deleted, local saved data may be removed under your device’s app-data rules.

## Membership and billing

The current app’s membership page is **informational only**. No Tanakh Anew subscription, trial, or purchase button exists, and no credit card is required to read. If an optional paid feature is added later, this page and the App Store listing will be updated to state the real price and restore/cancellation steps before launch. The Tanakh text will remain free to read.

See [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/tanakh-anew-privacy.md) and [Text Editions](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/tanakh-anew-sources.md).
