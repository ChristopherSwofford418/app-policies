# Milarah · מילארה — Help & Support

**Status: pre-release.** Milarah has a separate iOS app draft, but no Apple-validated Milarah TestFlight build or public App Store release has been verified yet. Do not pay anyone outside Apple's own purchase sheet for Milarah.

The planned first build provides a free, offline alphabet starter with English or Hebrew guidance. Its optional grown-up area offers expanded vowel/word lessons through a directly billed **Apple weekly subscription** and an optional, separate **Sign in with Apple** adult account. A child profile and cloud reading-progress sync are not offered. The adult-only math-and-reading pause is a parental gate, **not** proof of age or identity.

- **Read without paying:** Choose a guide language and tap the free alphabet starter. Do not sign in to read the free lessons.
- **Purchase or restore:** In the grown-up area, review the price shown by Apple's purchase sheet and use **Subscribe with Apple** or **Restore Apple purchases**. Availability of the paid product in an actual TestFlight build must still be verified on an iPhone. A declined or cancelled purchase does not remove the free starter.
- **Cancel a subscription:** Use [Apple's subscription management](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428). Signing out, deleting the adult account or uninstalling Milarah does **not** cancel Apple billing.
- **Sign out or delete an adult account:** The grown-up area includes these options after an adult has signed in with Apple. Deleting the adult account does not erase on-device lesson-completion marks; choose **Erase local reading progress** separately if desired. No child account is created.
- **Offline/data loss:** Reading cards and completion marks are device-local; reinstalling or erasing app data may remove progress. The optional adult account does not sync a child's progress.

For general feedback, [open a public Milarah support issue](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) with a title starting `Milara:`. **Public issues are visible to others; do not include a child's name, age, reading scores, photos, contact details, payment information, passwords, or account tokens.** For sensitive account-deletion assistance, use a private support route when available rather than posting private details in an issue.
