# Milara · מילארה — Help & Support

Milara is a **pre-release** idea for Hebrew/English parent-guided reading practice. There is no app binary or purchase available yet. This page records the concept and provides a route for early feedback; it does not promise a lesson set, speech assessment, school curriculum, free trial or subscription.

For general feedback, open a [public issue in the support repository](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) and prefix the title `Milara:`. **Issues are public. Do not include a child’s name, age, reading scores, photos, contact details, passwords, or any other personal information.**

[Privacy information](milara-privacy.md) · Proposed locales: Hebrew and English. A full product support and account-deletion process must be verified before a release is submitted.
