# Milarah · מילארה — Help & Support

**Status: pre-release.** Milarah 1.0.0 (3) is Apple-validated for **private internal TestFlight**. TestFlight availability is not App Store approval or a public release. The new build still requires native flow verification and App Review acceptance. Do not pay anyone outside Apple's own purchase sheet for Milarah.

Milarah offers a free offline 22-letter and five-final-form starter with English or Hebrew guidance and parent-guided shape comparisons. Optional adult-only Premium adds six bundled vowel/word lessons and pointed-word comparisons through a directly billed **weekly Apple subscription**. Optional **Sign in with Apple** is for an adult account, not a child profile. Reading progress stays on the device and does not sync to the adult account. The arithmetic pause before adult choices is a parental gate, **not** proof of age or identity.

- **Read without paying:** Choose a guide language, start the free letter path and try the shape-comparison mission. A child does not need an account or subscription for these routes.
- **Purchase or restore:** In the grown-up area, review the locally displayed Apple subscription price, then use **Subscribe with Apple** or **Restore Apple purchases**. A visible purchase prompt is not a completed transaction. A canceled or declined purchase does not remove the free starter.
- **Cancel:** Use [Apple's subscription management](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428). Signing out, deleting an adult account or uninstalling the app does **not** cancel Apple billing.
- **Adult account:** An adult may sign out or choose **Delete adult account** after signing in. Deleting that account does not erase local reading records. Use **Erase local reading progress** separately if desired.
- **Offline/data loss:** Free lessons, completed-lesson marks and shape-practice records are stored on this device; reinstalling or erasing app data may remove them. An optional adult account does not sync child progress.

For general feedback, [open a public Milarah support issue](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) with a title starting `Milara:`. **Issues are public. Do not include a child's name, age, reading records, photos, contact details, payment information, passwords or account tokens.** For a sensitive deletion or sign-in problem, create only a general issue asking for private follow-up and use the in-app account-deletion control when available. See the [Privacy Policy](milara-privacy.md).
