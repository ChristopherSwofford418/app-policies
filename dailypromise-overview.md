# Bible Anew: Verse & Prayer

*A quieter way back to Scripture. Formerly DailyPromise.*

Bible Anew is a Bible-reading and devotional iPhone app by ClearPass Technologies. **DailyPromise 1.1.4 is the version currently available**; the Bible Anew features described here are part of the planned **1.1.5 update**, not a promise that the update has already been released.

## The complete Bible, freely available

The 1.1.5 update brings the public-domain **66-book World English Bible** into an offline reader. Browse books and chapters, search the text, save a verse and share passages with their references. Bible reading does **not** require a subscription or an app account.

## A personal daily rhythm

Choose a focus and a real starting point in **John, Psalms or Proverbs**. A daily Scripture passage and an optional prayer/reflection check-in make it easier to return without streak pressure. Reading choices and saved verses stay in on-device app storage rather than syncing to an app account.

## Optional weekly membership

In Bible Anew 1.1.5, new purchasers can choose one auto-renewing **Bible Anew Weekly** membership for the full daily devotional reflection, a curated reflection archive and optional local daily-verse reminders. It costs **$2.99 per week in the United States**; Apple presents the actual local storefront price and any eligible introductory offer before purchase. There is **no guaranteed free trial**. Previously purchased monthly or annual subscriptions remain restorable while active. The full offline Bible stays free.
