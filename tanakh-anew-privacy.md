# Tanakh Anew · תנ״ך מחדש — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 30, 2026.** This page describes the Tanakh Anew 1.0 app candidate. The app is not yet available on the App Store.

## What the app keeps on your device

Your Hebrew/English selection, reading preferences, saved verses, and reading check-ins are stored locally on your device. The Hebrew and JPS 1917 Tanakh editions are bundled with the app so the reader and search work offline.

The current app has **no user account, cloud sync, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, location collection, document uploads, or in-app purchase**. We do not receive your saved verses or reading preferences from the app. We do not sell them or use them for targeted advertising.

## Deleting local data

Removing the app deletes its locally stored preferences, saved verses, and reading check-ins under your device’s normal app-data rules. There is no server-side Tanakh Anew account to delete in this version.

## Support and future changes

If you contact support outside the app, the information you choose to provide is handled by the contact service you use. Please do **not** post private reading notes, account details, or sensitive personal information in a public GitHub issue. [Help & Support](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/tanakh-anew-support.md) explains the current contact route.

If a future version adds an account, synchronization, analytics, or a paid feature, its behavior and App Store privacy answers must be reviewed and this policy updated **before** release. This document makes no claim about features that are not in the current candidate.

Tanakh Anew is an independent Jewish reading app and is not affiliated with Sefaria, JPS, or tanach.us. Text-edition provenance is documented in the app’s [source notes](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/tanakh-anew-sources.md).
