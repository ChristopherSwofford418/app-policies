# Tanakh Anew · תנ״ך מחדש — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 1, 2026.** This page describes the currently distributed **internal TestFlight 1.0.0/build 1**, not an unreleased source candidate. Tanakh Anew is not yet publicly available on the App Store.

## What build 1 keeps on your device

Your Hebrew/English selection, reading preferences, saved verses, and reading check-ins are stored locally on your device. The Hebrew and JPS 1917 Tanakh editions are bundled with the app so the reader and search work offline.

Build 1 has **no user account, cloud sync, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, location collection, document uploads, or in-app purchase**. We do not receive your saved verses or reading preferences from that build. We do not sell them or use them for targeted advertising.

## Deleting local data

Removing the app deletes its locally stored preferences, saved verses, and reading check-ins under your device’s normal app-data rules. There is no server-side Tanakh Anew account to delete in build 1.

## Support and future changes

If you contact support outside the app, the information you choose to provide is handled by the contact service you use. Please do **not** post private reading notes, account details, or sensitive personal information in a public GitHub issue. [Help & Support](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/tanakh-anew-support.md) explains the current contact route.

An **unbuilt future version** is being developed with optional Sign in with Apple and Apple-billed premium study through RevenueCat. Those features are **not present in build 1**. Before that version is released, this policy and the App Store privacy answers must be updated to explain its real account, identifier, purchase and third-party processing behavior after native-device verification. This page does not represent that candidate as shipped.

Tanakh Anew is an independent Jewish reading app and is not affiliated with Sefaria, JPS, or tanach.us. Text-edition provenance is documented in the app’s [source notes](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/tanakh-anew-sources.md).
