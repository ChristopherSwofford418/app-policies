# EnviroCheck — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 6, 2026.**

EnviroCheck lets you search for a city or, if you choose, use your device's foreground location to view available air-quality and current-weather readings. The app's composite indicator is informational; it **does not** test drinking water, pollen, noise, personal health, or safety.

## What is sent off the device

When you search for a city, the search text is sent over HTTPS to OpenWeatherMap's geocoding API. When you select a place or tap **Use My Location**, the selected latitude and longitude are sent over HTTPS to OpenWeatherMap's air-pollution and current-weather APIs so the app can display available readings. Your internet connection's network address is involved in these requests. The app requests iOS foreground-location permission only when you tap **Use My Location**; you can deny or revoke it in iOS Settings and continue searching by city.

[OpenWeather's published privacy notice](https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy) states that it **does not collect or store API request parameters, including locations, city names, IP addresses or user agents**, while retaining API usage counts by product. We do not control the provider's systems or future practices; consult its notice for current terms. EnviroCheck does not sell information or use these API requests for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Information kept on your device

The app stores up to five recent searched/selected locations, locations you explicitly bookmark after a successful reading, and a cache of displayed air-and-weather readings for up to 30 minutes. These are kept in app storage on your device, not uploaded to a publisher account service. You can remove individual bookmarked places in Saved. Deleting the app removes app-owned local storage from the device, subject to your device's backup/restore behavior. The app does not offer a separate in-app clearing control for recent searches or the score cache. It cannot erase data held by Apple, the weather provider or a device backup.

## Optional subscriptions

Monthly and Annual EnviroCheck Pro subscriptions use direct Apple StoreKit. The app reads Apple's active-purchase state to grant Pro features and provides **Restore Purchases**. Apple handles billing, cancellation, refunds and payment-card information. EnviroCheck does not receive or store your payment-card number and does not require a publisher account for its free or Pro features.

## Choices and contact

You may use city search without granting device location. You can manage foreground-location permission in iOS Settings and subscriptions in your Apple account. If you have a privacy question, use the publisher's [EnviroCheck support page](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/blob/main/envirocheck-support.md). Do not send passwords, card details or full purchase receipts by email.
