# Tanakh Anew · תנ״ך מחדש — Text editions

Tanakh Anew’s proposed offline 24-book Jewish Tanakh uses **two specifically identified public-domain editions**, not the unrestricted reuse of every edition hosted by a text library:

- **Hebrew:** *Tanach with Nikkud*, a vocalized Hebrew edition marked **Public Domain** in the per-edition [Sefaria Export](https://github.com/Sefaria/Sefaria-Export) metadata.
- **English:** *The Holy Scriptures: A New Translation (JPS 1917)*, a Jewish Publication Society 1917 English edition marked **Public Domain** in its per-edition export metadata. See also the [JPS translation history](https://jps.org/resources/tanakh-customer-guide/) and [eBible edition notice](https://ebible.org/engjps/copyright.htm).

The app bundles the traditional Jewish **24 books**, assembled from 39 source volumes per language. Some editions differ in verse numbering. This app does **not** bundle contemporary copyrighted translations or commentary, offer rabbinic rulings, or claim endorsement by Sefaria, JPS, or tanach.us.

This note describes the source candidate, not an already approved or released App Store binary.
