# RigSlate — Privacy Information (Pre-release)

RigSlate is a **pre-release concept** for RV departure/arrival notes, an owner-entered condition history, and a selective handoff packet. There is no downloadable app, user account, purchase, or subscription yet. RigSlate currently collects no data through an app because no app is operating; this does not describe how a future product will work.

A future version could store the owner’s rig nickname, custom checklists, dated status, optional photos/notes, and intentionally selected recipients for a handoff. No VIN, license plate, precise location, contacts access, background tracking, cloud sync, encryption, or sharing control is claimed to exist today. Before release, a policy and App Store privacy answers will explain data actually collected, service providers, storage/sync, retention, permissions, sharing, export and deletion. Avoid including identifying plates, home addresses, access codes or children in any future sample media.

For general questions, use the [public support route](rigslate-support.md). A public GitHub issue is visible to others under GitHub's privacy practices. **Do not post vehicle identifiers, registration papers, travel locations, photos, contact information, or access instructions in a public issue.**
