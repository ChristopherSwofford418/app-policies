# DorYachad · דוריחד — Privacy information

**Pre-release notice · September 30, 2026.** DorYachad is an App Store draft for proposed Hebrew-first family logistics around helping older parents. There is **no released DorYachad app**, account, subscription or app-collected family/health data at this time.

The product concept focuses on coordinating everyday visits, rides, appointments and responsibilities, **not** diagnosis, medical advice, medication decisions, electronic health records, eligibility or treatment. Before release, this notice will be replaced with an implementation-specific privacy policy describing what family information is stored locally or synchronized, who can access it, access revocation, encryption, retention/deletion, purchases and any processors. We will not claim secure cloud sharing or medical privacy compliance until implemented and tested.

For pre-release questions see [DorYachad support](doryachad-support.md). Please do not place personal, patient, medical or family details in the public issue tracker.
