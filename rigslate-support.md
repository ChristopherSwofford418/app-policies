# RigSlate — Help & Support (Pre-release)

RigSlate is a **pre-release** RV-owner condition and handoff concept. There is no downloadable app, purchase, subscription, or service assurance. A future checklist would not certify that a vehicle is roadworthy or replace manufacturer instructions, a qualified technician, roadside assistance or applicable law; do not rely on an app to decide whether a rig is safe to drive or tow.

For general feedback, [open a public issue in the support repository](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) with a title beginning `RigSlate:`. **Issues are public. Do not post license plates, VINs, home addresses, travel plans, vehicle or family photos, access codes, or personal information.** For an immediate safety issue, stop using the equipment and contact a qualified professional or the appropriate emergency service.

[Privacy information](rigslate-privacy.md). Functional support and any payment or cancellation guidance will be published only after an app is built and tested.
