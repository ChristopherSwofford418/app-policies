# CallNote Pro — Support

CallNote Pro helps you keep notes and searchable summaries from recordings and audio that you are authorized to capture or import. Some call-routing and provider-number features depend on account eligibility and service availability; **do not rely on CallNote for emergency calls or assume a provider number is available**. Get the consent required by your location and circumstances before recording or importing someone else's voice.

## Contact

Email [info@clearpasstechnologies.com](mailto:info@clearpasstechnologies.com) for help with your account, transcription/import, access to an eligible number, StoreKit subscriptions, Restore Purchases, privacy or account deletion. Include the app version/build, the screen involved and the exact error. Please **do not send recordings, transcripts, passwords, payment-card details, verification codes or complete purchase receipts** in an initial support email.

## Subscription help

Weekly and Annual plans are managed and billed by Apple. Check the plan shown in the app before purchase. If a completed purchase has not unlocked Pro, reopen the app and use **Restore Purchases**. To change or cancel a subscription, use your iPhone's Apple Account subscription settings. Displaying an Apple purchase sheet alone does not mean a transaction completed. For an App Store refund request, use [Apple's Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Audio and account help

If microphone or file access is denied, review the app's permissions in iOS Settings and retry with audio you are authorized to use. Transcription may require a network connection and can fail when the service is unavailable; keep the original audio until you verify a result. If an in-app account or recording deletion does not complete, **do not assume the associated stored audio has been erased**: contact the business support address above so the deletion problem can be investigated. Do not send sensitive audio through email unless a private, approved support channel has been arranged.

This page is a support route, not proof that a particular native build or paid flow has passed iPhone testing. For app data handling details, use the Privacy link inside the current app; if it is unavailable, request the current notice by email before sending recordings.
