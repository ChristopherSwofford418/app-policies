## What the app provides

EnviroCheck displays available OpenWeatherMap air-quality and current-weather readings for a searched city or a foreground location that you choose to share. It calculates a proprietary 0–100 informational air-and-weather indicator from available provider readings. The provider's air category uses its own **1–5 scale**, not the U.S. Air Quality Index. UV detail is shown only when the provider actually supplies it. The app **does not assess drinking-water quality or safety**, pollen, noise or personal health risk. It does not deliver environmental alerts or a certified safety rating.

Provider readings and location coverage can be delayed, absent, inaccurate or interrupted. When required data is missing, the app reports it as unavailable rather than inventing a reading. It is not medical, emergency, environmental compliance, travel or other professional advice. For health and safety decisions, consult official local authorities and advisories.

## Free and optional Pro features

Free access includes supported city search, a current informational air-and-weather indicator when readings are available, and refresh.
