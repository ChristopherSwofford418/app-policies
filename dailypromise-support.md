# Bible Anew — Help & Support

**Formerly DailyPromise.** Have a question, bug report or idea? Open **Help & Support** in the app or email [support@dailypromise.io](mailto:support@dailypromise.io). Include the app version and a short description of what happened, but **do not send private prayer details, passwords, payment information or other sensitive information**.

## Read the Bible for free

In Bible Anew iOS 1.1.5 and later, open **Explore → Read the complete Bible**. Browse all 66 books of the public-domain World English Bible, search text, open chapters and save verses offline. Neither a membership nor an app account is required. The older DailyPromise 1.1.4 app offers a smaller daily-verse experience; update to 1.1.5 when it becomes available for the complete offline reader.

## Start a weekly membership

New purchasers in Bible Anew 1.1.5 can choose a single auto-renewing weekly plan for the full daily reflections, curated reflection archive and optional local reminders. The U.S. customer price is **$2.99 per week**; the price shown in the app comes from your Apple storefront. Only Apple’s purchase sheet confirms your actual billing terms and eligibility for any introductory offer. There is **no guaranteed free trial**. You may choose free Bible access at the paywall. In DailyPromise 1.1.4, older monthly or annual purchase options may still be displayed.

## Restore and manage a purchase

Open **Settings → Restore a purchase** while using the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Previously purchased monthly or annual subscriptions remain eligible for restore if active. To manage or cancel billing, use **Settings → Manage with Apple** or [Apple’s subscriptions page](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). Signing out or deleting the optional Bible Anew app account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

## Apple Sign-In and deletion

After the first weekly-offer screen you may sign in with Apple or continue without an account. App account sign-in is separate from App Store purchase identity and does not sync your saved verses or reading choices. To permanently remove the app account, open **Settings → Delete app account** and confirm. For deletion assistance, email [support@dailypromise.io](mailto:support@dailypromise.io).
