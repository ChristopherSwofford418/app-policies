# KindShift — Help & Support (Pre-release)

KindShift is a **pre-release concept** for an invite-first, recurring volunteer rota. There is no downloadable app, purchase, subscription, or operating support team for this proposed product yet. It would not provide a public volunteer marketplace, child-safeguarding checks, legal compliance advice, or guaranteed shift coverage.

For general product feedback, [open a public issue in the support repository](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) with a title beginning `KindShift:`. **Issues are public: do not post volunteer names, contact information, shift locations/times, access codes, or other personal data.** Do not use public issues for urgent needs; contact your organization directly.

[Privacy information](kindshift-privacy.md). Product-specific support, deletion and billing instructions will be added only if an app is built and those capabilities exist.
