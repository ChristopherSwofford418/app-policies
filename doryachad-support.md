# DorYachad · דוריחד — Help & Support

DorYachad is a **pre-release** Hebrew-first concept for family coordination around helping older relatives. There is no downloadable app, purchase or subscription yet. The concept is an everyday logistics organizer, **not** a medical record, clinical decision tool, emergency service or medication-administration system.

For general feedback, [open a public issue in the support repository](https://github.com/ChristopherSwofford418/app-policies/issues/new) with a title beginning `DorYachad:`. **Issues are public. Do not post anyone’s health, medication, appointment, location, family or contact information.** Do not use this route for emergencies.

[Privacy information](doryachad-privacy.md) · Proposed locales: Hebrew and English. Functional support, family permissions, account deletion and billing information will be documented only after the corresponding app features are built and tested.
